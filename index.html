Learning modules

1. Mission Map

An interactive map of UN peacekeeping operations, special political missions, regional offices, envoys and support offices, with each mandate's geographic scope and a pre-deployment reflection.

2. Decision Scenarios

Branching field dilemmas, from community consultation to rumour response, with immediate feedback scored on four competencies.

3. Bias Toolkit

A field guide to common cognitive biases, the PAUSE protocol and an assumption-check worksheet to use before real decisions.

4. Team Debrief

Run a facilitated team discussion: gather perspectives, vote on options, compare with guidance and agree action points. Export the session to share it.

5. My Progress

See your competency profile across scenarios, revisit your reflections and export a learning summary.

What you'll practise

Each scenario choice is scored on four competencies, drawing on cultural intelligence (CQ) research, the humanitarian principles and Do No Harm practice.

Suggested learning path

  1. Orient: find your duty station on the mission map and read its mandate scope.
  2. Reflect: answer the pre-deployment questions on the mission panel.
  3. Practise: complete the linked scenarios and read the feedback.
  4. Internalise: learn the PAUSE protocol and use the assumption check before real decisions.
  5. Discuss: run a team debrief with colleagues, especially national staff.

About this prototype

The Navigator is an educational technology prototype developed for academic demonstration. It is not an official UN training product, and it does not measure or diagnose anyone's implicit bias.

Mission information is based on the UN field missions directory at unmissions.org. Each entry has a source link and a verification status. Humanitarian content draws on UN: Deliver Humanitarian Aid.

All scenarios are fictional composites and do not describe real communities.
