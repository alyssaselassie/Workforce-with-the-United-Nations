A note on measuring implicit bias

This toolkit builds awareness and decision habits. It does not test or score anyone's implicit bias. Reaction-time measures such as the Implicit Association Test can prompt useful reflection, but research cautions against using them to diagnose individuals or predict their behavior. Structural safeguards, such as diverse consultation, disaggregated data and deliberate pauses, are the more dependable protection.
