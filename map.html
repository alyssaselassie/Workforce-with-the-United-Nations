UN Field Mission Globe

Peacekeeping operations, special political missions, regional offices, envoys and support offices listed in the UN field missions directory. Select a marker, region or table row and the globe turns to that area, highlights the countries in the mandate and shows its scope, sources, photos and a pre-deployment reflection. Markers show headquarters or main offices, not full areas of operation.

Drag to turn the globe; scroll or use + and − to zoom. A number on a marker means several offices are close together. Map data: Natural Earth. Boundaries and names shown do not imply official endorsement or acceptance by the United Nations.
