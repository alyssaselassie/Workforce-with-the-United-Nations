Team Debrief

Run a facilitated discussion around a scenario: gather perspectives from across the team, record a group vote, compare it with the guidance and agree on action points. Built for one shared screen in a meeting room or video call.

Sessions stay on this device and reset when you refresh or reopen the site (demo mode). Nothing is sent to a server. To share with colleagues on other devices, export the session file and have them import it. Imported files are merged with matching sessions. Avoid recording names or details that could identify community members.

Facilitation norms

  • Invite national staff and junior colleagues to speak first, which reduces anchoring on senior views.
  • Ask someone to act as "devil's advocate" for each option.
  • Describe behavior and circumstances, not people's character.
  • Speak from your own experience. Don't speak for a whole group.
  • Keep it confidential: no identifiable community details.
  • Before voting, ask whose perspective is missing.
