[[Block:Introduction]]

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:INTRO]]
Workplace Relief Map Survey. This short, anonymous survey asks how comfortable you feel in five everyday workplace spaces, in relation to your gender, age, religious identity, ethnicity and social class. It never asks which of these you are. It has 26 multiple-choice questions and takes about 5 minutes. Every question needs an answer; you can always choose "Prefer not to say or not applicable".

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:PRIVACY]]
Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time. We do not ask for your name, email, staff ID or department, and responses are anonymized, so your IP address and location are not recorded. The survey asks how comfortable you feel in relation to your gender, age, religious identity, ethnicity and social class. It never asks which of these you are. Results are shown only as one combined map, and each point on it only when at least 10 people have answered it. Individual answers are never shown to anyone, including supervisors.

[[Block:1. Your desk or workstation]]

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:RM_INTRO]]
This survey maps where people feel at ease at work. Think about the past 3 months (or since you joined, if that is more recent). For each of five workplace spaces, say how comfortable you feel there in relation to five parts of who you are. For example, someone may feel at ease in meeting rooms in relation to their age, but uncomfortable in relation to their gender. These questions never ask which gender, age, religion, ethnicity or social class you are, only how comfortable you feel. If you prefer not to answer, or you do not use a space, choose "Prefer not to say or not applicable".
