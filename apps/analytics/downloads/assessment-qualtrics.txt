[[Block:Introduction]]

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:INTRO]]
UN Workforce Inclusion & Psychological Safety Assessment. This assessment asks about psychological safety, workplace inclusion, cross-cultural collaboration, leadership and management, awareness of bias in workplace practices and organizational support, and ends with a Relief Map of how comfortable you feel in five workplace settings. It has 61 multiple-choice questions and takes about 11 minutes. Every question needs an answer; if a statement does not apply to you, choose "Don't know or not applicable" (in the Relief Map section, "Prefer not to say or not applicable").

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:PRIVACY]]
Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time before you submit; nothing is recorded until you do. We do not ask for your name, email, staff ID, entity or duty station. Your response is stored without your IP address or location. The Relief Map section asks how comfortable you feel in relation to your gender, age, religious identity, ethnicity and social class. It never asks which of these you are, and you can always choose "Prefer not to say". Your department is used only to group answers, and the date only to assign a reporting period (such as October to December 2026). The exact date and time are not stored. Results are shown only for groups of at least 10 people, and smaller groups are protected from being worked out from other results. Individual answers are never shown to anyone, including supervisors.
