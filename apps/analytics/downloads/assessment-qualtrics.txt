[[ID:INTRO]]
UN Workforce Inclusion & Psychological Safety Assessment. This assessment asks about psychological safety, workplace inclusion, cross-cultural collaboration, leadership and management, awareness of bias in workplace practices, and organizational support. It has 36 multiple-choice questions and takes about 8 minutes. Every question needs an answer; if a statement does not apply to you, choose "Don't know or not applicable".

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:PRIVACY]]
Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time before you submit; nothing is recorded until you do. We do not ask for your name, email, staff ID, entity or duty station. Your response is stored without your IP address or location. Your department is used only to group answers, and the date only to assign a reporting period (such as October to December 2026). The exact date and time are not stored. Results are shown only for groups of at least 10 people, and smaller groups are protected from being worked out from other results. Individual answers are never shown to anyone, including supervisors.
