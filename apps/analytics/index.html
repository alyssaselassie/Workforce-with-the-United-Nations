Relief Map

How respondents are protected
  • Everyone's answers form one combined map. The survey does not ask for a department, and nobody is asked which gender, age, religion, ethnicity or social class they are.
  • Nothing is shown until at least 10 people have taken part, and a point on the map only when at least 10 people have answered it ("Prefer not to say" does not count).
  • Individual answers never reach this page: live results are combined on a secure server first.
  • Demonstration data is fictional and is never mixed with real results.
