Results file

For researchers running the assessment in Qualtrics: open an export (CSV) or an aggregated results file (JSON). It is read in this browser and never uploaded.

Privacy: results are shown only for groups of at least 10 completed responses, smaller groups are protected from being worked out by subtraction, and individual answers are never displayed.
