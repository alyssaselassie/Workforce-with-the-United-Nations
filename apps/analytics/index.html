How respondents are protected
  • Nothing is shown for a department, or for all departments together, until it has at least 10 responses in the period.
  • If showing a department would let someone work out a smaller group's answers by subtraction, it is hidden too.
  • Individual answers never reach this page: live results are combined on a secure server first.
  • Demonstration data is fictional and is never mixed with real results.
