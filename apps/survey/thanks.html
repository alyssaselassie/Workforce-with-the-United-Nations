What happens next

  • Your answers are combined with everyone else's into one Relief Map. It is shown only once at least 10 people have taken part, and only to authorized researchers and project administrators.
  • Nobody, including your supervisor, can see your individual answers.
  • Because the response cannot be linked to you, it cannot be changed or withdrawn after submission.

You can close this page now. If you share this device, there is nothing to clear: the survey does not keep your answers in this browser.
