Workplace Relief Map Survey

Where at work do people feel at ease, and where not? This short, anonymous survey asks how comfortable you feel in five everyday workplace spaces, in relation to your gender, age, religious identity, ethnicity and social class. Everyone's answers are combined into one Relief Map.

What the survey covers

Five workplace spaces, one page each. On each page you say how comfortable you feel there in relation to five parts of who you are: your gender, age, religious identity, ethnicity and social class. The survey never asks which of these you are.

  • 1

    Your desk or workstation

    Where you do most of your work, including open-plan areas.

  • 2

    Meeting rooms

    Rooms where meetings and presentations take place.

  • 3

    Your supervisor's office

    Or wherever you usually meet your supervisor in person.

  • 4

    Kitchen, break room or cafeteria

    Shared spaces where people eat, take breaks and chat.

  • 5

    Hallways, elevators and entrances

    The shared spaces you pass through every day.

How it works

  1. Agree to take part. You can stop at any time before you submit.
  2. Answer five short pages, one for each space. Every question needs an answer; "Prefer not to say or not applicable" is always available.
  3. Review and submit. Check or change any answer before you send it.

Your privacy

  • Taking part is voluntary. Nothing is recorded until you submit.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, staff ID or department. Your response is stored without your IP address, your location or the date and time you took part.
  • The survey never asks which gender, age, religion, ethnicity or social class you are.
  • Results are shown only as one combined map, once at least 10 people have taken part. Individual answers are never shown to anyone, including supervisors.
