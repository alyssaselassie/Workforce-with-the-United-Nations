UN Workforce Inclusion & Psychological Safety Assessment

How safe is it to speak up where you work? Do people feel included, supported and treated fairly? This short, anonymous assessment asks about your experience at work, so that organizations can see where practices help and where they need attention.

Start the assessment Read the privacy statement
  • About 8 minutesOne sitting, on any device
  • 36 questionsAll multiple choice, all required
  • AnonymousNo names, emails or staff IDs
  • Group results onlyGroups of 10 or more people

What the assessment covers

Six short sections, with the same answer options throughout each section.

  • A

    Psychological Safety

    Whether you can speak up, ask questions and admit mistakes without fear.

  • B

    Workplace Inclusion

    Belonging, fair treatment and equal access to opportunities.

  • C

    Cross-Cultural Collaboration

    Your confidence in working across cultures, languages and duty stations.

  • D

    Leadership & Management

    Fairness, openness and respect from supervisors and management.

  • E

    Implicit Bias Awareness

    How often you notice bias in workplace practices. It is not a test of anyone's own bias.

  • F

    Organizational Support

    Training, reporting channels and support for inclusion and development.

How it works

  1. Agree to take part. You can stop at any time before you submit.
  2. Choose your department. It is the only question about you, and it is used only to group answers.
  3. Answer six short sections. Every question needs an answer; "Don't know or not applicable" is always available.
  4. Review and submit. Check or change any answer before you send it.

Your privacy

  • Taking part is voluntary. Nothing is recorded until you submit.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, staff ID, entity or duty station. Your response is stored without your IP address or location.
  • Only the reporting period is kept, never the date or time you took part.
  • Results are reported only for groups of at least 10 people. Individual answers are never shown to anyone, including supervisors.
