Privacy statement

This statement explains what the Workplace Relief Map Survey collects, where it is kept, who can see results and how your anonymity is protected.

What the survey is for

The survey shows where people feel at ease at work and where they do not, in five everyday workplace spaces. Everyone's answers are combined into one Relief Map, which is used to learn and to improve workplaces and is never used to assess individuals.

What we collect

  • Your answers to 26 multiple-choice questions, stored as answer codes: how comfortable you feel in five workplace spaces in relation to your gender, age, religious identity, ethnicity and social class. Nothing else.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, staff ID, department, entity, duty station or nationality.
  • Which gender, age, religion, ethnicity or social class you are. The survey asks only how comfortable you feel in relation to each of them.
  • The date and time you took part, your IP address or your location.
  • Written comments. Every question is multiple choice, so you cannot accidentally identify yourself in free text.
  • Cookies or tracking. This website loads no advertising, tracking tools, fonts or scripts from other companies.

Where your answers are stored

When you submit, your response is sent over an encrypted connection to a secure database. The database accepts complete, valid responses only, and it does not allow anyone to read individual responses through the website or its public connection.

Like most online services, the database provider keeps short-lived technical logs, which may include IP addresses, to keep the service secure. These logs are not part of the survey data and are not used in any analysis.

This copy is a demonstration. It is not connected to a database, so your answers are not collected or sent anywhere. When you submit, they are kept only in this browser, labelled as demonstration data, and you can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Who can see the results

  • Only authorized researchers and project administrators, who must sign in, and only as one combined Relief Map for everyone who took part.
  • Nothing is shown until at least 10 people have taken part, and each point on the map is shown only when at least 10 people have answered it ("Prefer not to say" does not count).
  • Individual answers are never shown or exported, including to supervisors or managers.

Your choices

  • Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time before you submit, and nothing is recorded until you do.
  • Every question offers "Prefer not to say or not applicable".
  • Because your response cannot be linked to you, it cannot be found, changed or withdrawn after you submit it.

Responsibility, retention and contact

To be completed by the organization running the survey, before real responses are collected: who is responsible for the data, how long responses are kept and when they are deleted, and who to contact with questions.

These details depend on that organization's own data protection policy.
