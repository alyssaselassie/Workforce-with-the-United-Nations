Privacy statement

This statement explains what the UN Workforce Inclusion & Psychological Safety Assessment collects, where it is kept, who can see results and how your anonymity is protected.

What the assessment is for

The assessment helps organizations understand, department by department, how safe people feel to speak up, how included and supported they feel, and where workplace practices need attention. Results are used for organizational learning and are never used to assess individuals.

What we collect

  • Your department, chosen from a list of 12.
  • Your answers to 61 multiple-choice questions (as answer codes). In the Relief Map section these are how comfortable you feel in five workplace settings in relation to your gender, age, religious identity, ethnicity and social class.
  • The reporting period (for example, October to December 2026), assigned when you submit. The exact date and time are not stored.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, staff ID, entity, duty station or nationality.
  • Which gender, age, religion, ethnicity or social class you are. The Relief Map asks only how comfortable you feel in relation to each of them.
  • Your IP address or location, which are not part of your response.
  • Written comments. Every question is multiple choice, so you cannot accidentally identify yourself in free text.
  • Cookies or tracking. This website loads no advertising, tracking tools, fonts or scripts from other companies.

Where your answers are stored

When you submit, your response is sent over an encrypted connection to a secure database. The database accepts complete, valid responses only, and it does not allow anyone to read individual responses through the website or its public connection.

Like most online services, the database provider keeps short-lived technical logs, which may include IP addresses, to keep the service secure. These logs are not part of the assessment data and are not used in any analysis.

This copy is a demonstration. It is not connected to a database, so your answers are not collected or sent anywhere. When you submit, they are kept only in this browser, labelled as demonstration data, and you can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Who can see the results

  • Only authorized researchers and project administrators, who must sign in, and only as aggregated group results.
  • No result is shown for any group of fewer than 10 responses. Where a small group could be worked out by comparing other results, those other results are held back too.
  • On the Relief Map, each point is shown only when at least 10 people in the group answered it ("Prefer not to say" does not count), and points are held back together in the same way, so none can be worked out from the others.
  • Individual answers are never shown or exported, including to supervisors or managers.

Your choices

  • Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time before you submit, and nothing is recorded until you do.
  • Every question offers "Don't know or not applicable"; in the Relief Map section, "Prefer not to say or not applicable".
  • Because your response cannot be linked to you, it cannot be found, changed or withdrawn after you submit it.

Responsibility, retention and contact

To be completed by the organization running the assessment, before real responses are collected: who is responsible for the data, how long responses are kept and when they are deleted, and who to contact with questions.

These details depend on that organization's data protection policy (for UN entities, the UN Personal Data Protection and Privacy Principles and the entity's own rules).
