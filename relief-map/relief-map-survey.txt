[[Block:Introduction]]

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:INTRO]]
Relief Maps: Workplace Experience Survey. This short survey asks how comfortable, included and heard you feel in six everyday workplace settings, such as team meetings, online work and decision-making. Answers from the whole team are combined into a Relief Map that shows where people feel at ease and where there may be barriers. It takes about 5 minutes.

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:PRIVACY]]
Taking part is voluntary: you can skip any question and stop at any time. The survey is anonymous: it does not ask for your name, email or staff ID, and it does not record your IP address or location. Individual answers are never shared with supervisors or anyone else in the organization. Results are reported only as group averages, and never for groups of fewer than 5 people. Questions about the survey: [research team email].
