[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:INTRO]]
Relief Maps: Workplace Experience Survey. This short survey asks how you feel in six everyday workplace settings, from team meetings to decision-making. Think about the past 3 months, or since you started if that is more recent. Your answers are combined with your team's to make a Relief Map that shows where people feel safe, included and at ease. It takes about 5 minutes.

[[Question:DB]]
[[ID:PRIVACY]]
Taking part is voluntary, and you can skip any question. Your answers are anonymous: we do not collect your name, email, staff ID, IP address or location. Supervisors never see individual answers. Results are shared only as group averages, never for groups of fewer than 5 people. Questions: [team contact email].
