    How it works

    The survey

    1. Welcome and consent. "No" ends the survey.
    2. Six workplace settings, one page each, with 3 statements: one for each line on the map.
    3. Workplace overall: 5 statements.
    4. About you (optional): 5 questions, each with "Prefer not to answer".
    5. Your ideas (optional): 2 open questions.
    6. Thank you.

    Scoring

    • Answers count 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). All statements are positive, so higher is always better.
    • Each point on the map is the average answer to one statement.
    • Skipped questions are left out, never counted as 0 or 3.

    Use your own results

    In Qualtrics, export the responses as a CSV file with Use numeric values, then choose Load your Qualtrics export above.

    Privacy

    • No names, emails, staff IDs, IP addresses or locations.
    • Nothing is shown for fewer than 5 people.
    • Groups are compared one question at a time. If the people left out of a comparison number 1 to 4, it is hidden too.
    • Comments are never shown.
